1. Who we are and scope

SOS Game Pro (the “App”) is a mobile game developed and published by mdalamin97 (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, who it is shared with, and what choices you have. The App is intended for children and families aged 3 and older. It applies to the App on Android devices. By installing and using the App you acknowledge this policy.

2. Information we do not collect

The App does not require registration or login. We do not collect or request:

  • your name, email address, phone number, postal address or any account credentials;
  • precise or background location (GPS);
  • contacts, SMS or call logs, calendar, photos, videos, files or documents;
  • camera or microphone data;
  • payment or financial information (the App has no real-money purchases; shop items are unlocked by watching optional rewarded ads).

Player names you may type into the on-screen score fields (for example “Player 1”) are used only while the match is being played on your device. We do not send them to any server and we do not receive them.

We do not operate our own servers for this App, and we do not run our own analytics or crash-reporting tools.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a small amount of data in the App’s private local storage on your device only. This includes:

  • which shop items (board, background, skin and special board themes) you have unlocked and which ones you have equipped;
  • how many rewarded ads you have watched toward unlocking an item;
  • your sound on/off setting;
  • your best score.

This data is not transmitted to us and is not visible to us. It is removed when you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. Because Android’s automatic backup may be enabled on your device, your device’s own backup service (for example Google backup) may also store a copy of this local data under your own account and its own policies.

4. Advertising and data collected by Google AdMob

The App is free and supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), a service of Google LLC, to show two kinds of ads:

  • Interstitial ads – full-screen ads that may appear after a match ends;
  • Rewarded ads – optional video ads you can choose to watch to unlock shop items. You are never forced to watch a rewarded ad.

Because the App is intended for children and families aged 3 and older, ad requests and the advertising SDK must be configured to meet Google Play Families requirements. This includes using eligible Families-compliant ad SDKs and age-appropriate ads, and preventing personalised advertising and prohibited identifier transmission for children or users of unknown age, as required by applicable policy. The policy text alone does not configure the SDK; the app implementation must do so.

5. Your ad choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If consent is required where you live, the App should present an appropriate consent message before using advertising that requires consent. Where Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is implemented and configured, you can make or change your choice through the consent/privacy options shown in the App. Availability of these controls may depend on your region and the app version.

6. Fonts and network requests

The App needs an internet connection to load ads. The App’s start page may also request the “Poppins” typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). If this request occurs, Google may receive standard connection data such as your IP address and device/browser details, as described in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ. The App does not make any other network requests to our own or other third-party servers.

8. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell your personal information. Third-party Google services used by the App may receive information when they are requested: Google AdMob for advertising, and Google Fonts if the app requests the Poppins typeface, as described in Section 6. These services process connection or advertising data under their own privacy practices. We may also disclose information if required by law, court order or to protect rights and safety, although we hold no user account data that could be disclosed.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: kept until you clear the App’s data (Settings → Apps → SOS Game Pro → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the App.
  • Data held by Google AdMob: retained and deleted according to Google’s policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and manage ad data at myactivity.google.com.
  • Data held by us: we do not store personal data about you on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, contact us (Section 15) and we will respond.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for children and families, including children aged 3 and older. We aim to protect children’s privacy. The App does not require children to create an account or submit their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or precise location. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device. The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Advertising SDKs may process technical information such as device and app information, IP address, ad interactions and identifiers as described in Section 4. Because the App is intended for children and families, the app developer must configure ad requests and SDKs to meet Google Play Families requirements, including child-directed treatment, age-appropriate ad content and restrictions on advertising identifiers and personalised advertising. This policy describes the intended data practices; the actual app configuration must also comply with these requirements. Parents or guardians who have questions or believe that information has been submitted to us can contact us using the email in Section 15. We do not operate an account system or our own server for player data.

11. Security

Game data is kept in the App’s private storage on your device and is protected by Android’s app sandbox. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keeping your device updated is the best protection.

12. International data transfers

Google may process ad-related data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under Google’s own safeguards and terms. We do not transfer any personal data ourselves.

Because we do not hold personal data about you, the practical way to exercise these rights is through your device and Google controls described in Section 5, or by contacting us.
